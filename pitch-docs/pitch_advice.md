# SoftLogic Legal OS - Pitch Advice

Here is a guide to pitching the system to Sam Ogola & Co. Advocates.

## 1. Core Pitch Strategy
When presenting, avoid talking too much about code or technical jargon. Focus on:
- **Time Saved:** Automating routine status updates on WhatsApp will save the secretary hours of phone calls.
- **Clarity & Trust:** Clients will love getting automatic, professional milestone updates directly on WhatsApp without needing to call.
- **Organization:** A single source of truth for leads, cases, payments, and files.

## 2. Dynamic Pricing & Phases
Do not present the entire project as a single, massive KES 200,000+ package. That triggers sticker shock. Instead, break it down into phases. This lowers the barrier to entry and lets Sam see results before committing to the next stage.

### Phase 1: High-Performance Website & Lead Gen
- **Scope:** 4 pages (Home, About, Practice Areas, Contact), custom Navy/Red/Gold design, responsive mobile nav, basic contact form, floating WhatsApp button, local SEO setup, domain email config.
- **Price Range:** **KES 35,000 – KES 50,000**
- **Timeline:** 2-3 Weeks

### Phase 2: Operations Engine & Client Portals
- **Scope:** Custom database integration (Express + SQLite), web-based Stamp Duty Calculator, digital PDF property buying checklist (Lead Magnet), and client Case Verification portal.
- **Price Range:** **KES 60,000 – KES 90,000**
- **Timeline:** 3-4 Weeks

### Phase 3: The Digital Clerk (WhatsApp Bot + Admin Dashboard)
- **Scope:** Twilio/WhatsApp API integration, automated intake dialogues, secure internal Secretary Dashboard, M-Pesa push integration for consultation payments, KDPA logs.
- **Price Range:** **KES 70,000 – KES 110,000**
- **Timeline:** 4 Weeks

### Monthly Maintenance Plan
- **Scope:** Hosting support, domain renewals, daily database backups, minor content edits, security patches.
- **Price Range:** **KES 4,000 – KES 6,000 / month** (or billed annually)

## 3. Infrastructure & Costs
- **Response:** *"We keep infrastructure costs lean. By utilizing efficient technology like SQLite and Cloudflare, we can host the entire system for as little as KES 2,000 to 4,000 per month. You only pay for what your firm actively uses."*
